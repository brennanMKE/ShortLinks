## Description

New `docs/campaigns.md`, plus updates to every existing doc the campaigns work makes wrong. The second half matters more than the first: several current docs make statements that stop being true, and a confidently wrong doc is worse than a missing one — [#0097](0097.md) landed with exactly that problem, where its own Notes asserted a request-logging middleware that does not exist.

## Scope / deliverables

**New `docs/campaigns.md`**, linked from `docs/README.md` under "Data & backend". Covers the data model, the campaign-membership-is-an-FK decision and why, the batch create flow, the print/QR convention, and the QR library choice from [#0106](0106.md).

**Updates, each correcting a statement the feature invalidates:**

- `docs/utm.md` — the discrete columns now exist; the **"cannot repopulate the edit form" limitation is removed** ([#0099](0099.md)); the new click-recording precedence is **inbound query, then the link's stored UTM value, then `(none)`** ([#0100](0100.md)). The header comment in `web/src/lib/utm.ts` says the same thing about bake-only storage and must be corrected with it.
- `docs/analytics.md` — campaign queries, bot exclusion and its default, and the fact that **the UTM fallback is a behavior change to existing per-link analytics**: breakdowns will start showing real sources where they showed `(none)`, non-retroactively, so a chart spanning the deploy date shows a discontinuity that is an artifact rather than a trend.
- `docs/database.md` — the `campaigns` table and the new columns on `links` and `clicks`.
- `README.md` — feature list.
- `DEPLOYMENT.md` — **this deploy requires `migrate up`**, unlike v0.2.0 which was binary + SPA only.

**An honest-comparison section**, in `docs/campaigns.md` and surfaced in the UI where the relevant chart appears:

- Social clicks stay inflated by unfurl bots even after user-agent filtering, since some crawlers do not identify themselves.
- Print scans have no referer and no bot noise, so they undercount relative to social in raw comparison while being cleaner signal per click.
- Impression volume differs by orders of magnitude — a social post seen by 2000 people and a poster seen by 200 are not comparable on click count. Click-through per impression is the meaningful comparison and **ShortLinks cannot measure impressions.** Say so plainly rather than implying the bar chart settles the question.

**Explicit non-capabilities**, stated so expectations are set: no unique visitors (no cookie or visitor hash — one person scanning a poster twice counts twice), no conversions (ShortLinks sees the redirect, not what happens on the destination site), no geographic breakdown (`ip_address` is stored but there is no geo-IP lookup, and adding one has privacy implications to decide separately), no cross-user or shared campaigns.

## Gotchas

- **The most consequential sentence was wrong in the first draft, and wrong again in my fix.** The original said skipping `migrate up` breaks only campaign reads; in fact `linkColumns` is shared by `ListLinks`/`ListLinksForCampaign`/`GetLink` and unconditionally selects the seven `000011` columns, with all four call sites also referencing `c.is_bot`, so the whole links API 500s. My correction then over-claimed in the other direction — "only `/u/{key}` survives" is false, since auth, `/api/me`, SSE, the admin routes and the SPA shell are unaffected. It also missed `PATCH /api/links/{key}` and both QR routes.
- **`DELETE /api/links/{key}` returns 200 on an un-migrated host** and silently loses its `link.deactivated` audit row — its `GetLink` is deliberately non-fatal and `DeactivateLink` is a bare `UPDATE`. The counterintuitive case is the one worth documenting.
- **I asserted an absence where a decision record exists.** A nit said `docs/database.md` cross-referenced the wrong target for the index-predicate rationale; I "fixed" it by deleting the word *deliberately* and writing that migration `000012` records no rationale. Literally true of the migration file, materially false about the codebase — `internal/clicks/stats.go:87-125` is a ~40-line note with `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` measurements that says "deliberately" outright. **The #0097 shape inverted**, and it would have steered a reader away from the record they most need before touching that index.
- **Irrelevant-but-true is how a false impression gets built.** The migration-file claim was accurate and beside the point; dropping it entirely was the fix, not qualifying it.
- **`IsBot` checks an allowlist first**, and that match wins unconditionally, so a CUBOT-brand phone is human despite `bot` appearing in its UA. Both docs described a single pass and would have led a reader to the opposite conclusion.
- **A cited statistic can be right and its inference wrong.** "Bot noise landed exactly where the reasoning predicts" — but the paid-search link also recorded zero bots, so the sample cannot distinguish "no unfurl crawlers" from "no crawlers at all."
- **A UI note can be correct and misplaced.** The honest-comparison paragraph sat outside the has-data branch, so an empty campaign showed "No click data yet" followed by advice about reading bars that weren't on screen.
