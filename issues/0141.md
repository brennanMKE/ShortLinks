# 0141 — Agents can't get a click breakdown or time series across all links, only per link or per campaign

## Description

Add two reports: `breakdown` (clicks by one dimension, with share) and `timeseries` (daily or weekly clicks, optionally split by a dimension). These answer "where do my clicks come from?" and "how did Bluesky trend this month?" across all of a user's links, under the shared filters.

## Long Description

**`breakdown`**, extra params `dimension` (required): `source`, `medium`, `utm_campaign`, `content`, `term`, `referer`, `placement`, `campaign`, `link`, or `content_group` (uses #0140's key). `limit` defaults to 20, and the rest are summed into an `other` row.

```json
{"dimension": "source", "total": 1284, "rows": [{"value": "mastodon", "clicks": 498, "share": 0.388, "previous_clicks": 410}], "other": {"clicks": 12, "values": 4}}
```

Referrers are reported as hostnames (parse `clicks.referer`; empty means `(direct)`). Reuse and generalise `dimensionBreakdown` (`internal/clicks/stats.go:1014`) to take a predicate builder instead of one column/id pair, rather than writing a second copy.

**`timeseries`**, extra params `granularity=day|week` (default `day`; weeks start Monday in `tz`), `split_by` (any `breakdown` dimension) and `series_limit` (default 5, other series summed as `other`).

```json
{"granularity": "day", "series": [{"name": "mastodon", "points": [{"date": "2026-10-20", "clicks": 12}]}], "previous": [{"date": "2026-09-20", "clicks": 9}]}
```

Without `split_by` there is one series named `all`. Days with no clicks are zero-filled.

## Acceptance criteria

- Both reports are registered, catalogued and DB-tested, including zero-fill, week bucketing across a DST change, `other` rollups, referrer host parsing, and the `campaign`, `link` and `content_group` dimensions.
- `dimensionBreakdown` is generalised, and the existing per-link and campaign stats tests still pass unchanged.
- Documented in [docs/analytics.md](../docs/analytics.md).
- `go test ./...` passes.
